Commas, line breaks, and bullet points all work. Slashes are treated as INCI synonyms.

Important to know: Pore-clogging (comedogenicity) ratings come primarily from rabbit ear tests — an animal model more sensitive than human skin. An ingredient flagged here doesn't guarantee it will clog YOUR pores. Ratings vary with concentration, and a finished product may behave differently than its individual ingredients. Use this as a starting point, not a final verdict. When in doubt, patch test on a small area first.
How is this score calculated?

The product score starts at 10 and subtracts points for each flagged ingredient.

Concentration matters: Cosmetic labels list ingredients in descending order of concentration. The first 7 ingredients are typically present at meaningful levels (>1%). After that, ingredients drop below 1% and can appear in any order — making their concentration harder to gauge.

A flagged ingredient at position 2 counts much more in the score than the same ingredient at position 20. Look for the 🏷️ Top 7 badge on results in the high-concentration zone.

Strongly evidenced pore-cloggers count more than uncertain ones. Short lists (1-2 ingredients) with a strong clogger are penalized more since that ingredient likely dominates the formula.
